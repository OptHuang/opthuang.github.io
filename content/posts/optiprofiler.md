## Overview

**OptiProfiler** is a benchmarking platform for optimization solvers, with a current focus on derivative-free optimization. It is designed to make solver comparisons easier to run, reproduce, and inspect.

The project is under active development, and the accompanying manuscript is still being prepared. I will add a fuller technical description once the public version is ready.
